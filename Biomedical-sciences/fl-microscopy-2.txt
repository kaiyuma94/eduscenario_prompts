Fluorescence Microscopy Procedure
Base Procedure (Correct Protocol)
Cell Preparation:

Seed HEK293T cells on glass coverslips.
Fix cells in 4 percent PFA, following standard procedures.
Permeabilization and Blocking:

Wash cells three times with PBS.
Permeabilize cells with 0.1 percent Triton-X100 in PBS for 5 min at room temperature.
Block cells in 3 percent BSA in PBS for 30 min at room temperature.
Staining:

Stain cells with phalloidin-iFluor 488 in blocking solution for 1 hour at room temperature, protected from light.
Washing and Mounting:

Wash cells three times in PBS.
Mount coverslips on glass slides using DAPI-containing mounting medium.
Dry slides and seal with clear nail polish.
Imaging:

Use fluorescence microscopy to visualize phalloidin green and DAPI blue.
